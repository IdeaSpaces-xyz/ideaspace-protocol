# Working Here

## The rhythm

A session has a natural shape, and none of it needs managing:

1. **Arrive.** The whiteboard is read for you. Your assistant starts oriented — what this place
   is, what's active, what changed since last time.
2. **Work.** Think, write, research, build — the ordinary work, in the ordinary way.
3. **Save at the right moments.** When something crystallizes, say "save this" — or your
   assistant offers, once, at a natural pause. Not every thought gets saved; understanding
   settles at boundaries, not per message.
4. **Wrap.** At the end of meaningful work, the whiteboard gets updated if direction moved — so
   the next session (yours, or anyone's) arrives oriented.

## Ask in your own words

There is no command language to learn. "Save this." "Where were we?" "Share it with Anna."
"Put this online." "Anything new from the team?" Your assistant maps what you say to what the
space can do. The jobs in `guide-jobs` are the vocabulary — but your phrasing is fine.

## When it acts, and when it checks

One rule governs everything: **the more an action can reach beyond your machine, the more
deliberately it happens.**

- **Working and drafting** — silent. No ceremony for ordinary edits.
- **Saving what you asked for** — instant, then narrated: "Saved the pricing decision." A save is
  one step from undone, so it doesn't interrupt you to ask twice.
- **Anything that leaves your machine** — putting a space online, making it public, sharing it
  with someone — always shows you the plan first: exactly what would happen, before anything
  does. Nothing outward runs until you've seen it and said yes.

If your assistant ever seems to hesitate before an outward step, that's this rule working — not
uncertainty.

## Where structure comes from

Don't build empty folders for a future you imagine. Save real things; move them when a shape
suggests itself; let rooms grow their own whiteboards when a corner of the work develops its own
direction. The space ends up organized the way the work actually went — which is the organization
that helps.
